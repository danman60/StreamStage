# Carousel decks, both campaigns

Drafted 2026-08-17. Eight decks, four per campaign, one per rotation week.
Captions that carry these live in `recital-captions.md` and `contentday-captions.md`.

**Design system**, from `image-prompts.md` so these match everything else:

- Background near black, `#0A0A0A` to `#1A1A1A`. One accent only, cyan `#4EC5D4`. Amber `#F59E0B`
  for the Early Bird slide and nowhere else.
- Heading font Outfit, bold, tight leading. Generous negative space.
- 1080x1350 (4:5). Carousels reach followers first, so these are the authority format, not reach.
- **Generated images are backgrounds and textures only.** Never a fake dancer, studio, audience or
  testimonial. Any real person comes from real footage in `video/` or `posters/`.
- No em dashes or en dashes on any slide.
- Slide 1 has to work as a thumbnail. One idea, six words or fewer where possible.

## Production notes

- Seven slides each. Long enough to build an argument, short enough that people reach the CTA.
- Slide 1 is the thumbnail. If it needs two lines to make sense, it is too long.
- The final slide is the only one with a URL on it. Repeating the link on every slide reads as an
  ad rather than a point of view.
- Nothing on any slide states a client count, a percentage, a satisfaction figure or a turnaround
  faster than the published ten days.
- When a deck loops in a later rotation, change slide 1 and keep the argument. The thumbnail is what
  people recognise as a repeat.
