# Recital campaign, caption bank

Drafted 2026-08-17. Sixteen slots, four weeks, repeats indefinitely.
Assets and constraints per `ASSET-MANIFEST.md` and `recital-campaign-PLAYBOOK.md`.

**Rules applied to every caption below:**

- No em dashes or en dashes.
- No invented statistics, client counts, prices or availability numbers.
- Quotes are verbatim from `recital/quotes/quote-sheet.md`. Consent is handled, names run attached.
- The revenue line stays a reframe. You charge the media fee, we bill per dancer, the difference is
  yours. Nothing about who collects from families or how the money settles.
- Link in bio and in stories is `streamstage.live/recitals`. Never the calculator URL.
- **The Early Bird story in week 2 is the only dated item in the rotation.** When December 31 2026
  passes, either replace the date or pull that slot.
- Hashtags are a small fixed set, not a wall. Reels get reach from the hook, not from tags.

**Standard tag block:** `#danceteacher #dancestudioowner #recital #danceteacherlife #dancemom
#ontariodance`

## Notes

- Every reel opens on a line that works with the sound off, because most of them will be watched
  that way.
- Week 1 Reel A is the strongest post in the rotation and it should stay in the week 1 slot on
  every loop. It argues the whole case with no copy.
- No caption anywhere claims a number of studios, a satisfaction rate, or a turnaround faster than
  the ten days already published.
- When the rotation loops, vary the hook line and keep the body. The audience that saw week 1 last
  month is not the audience seeing it now, but the ones who did see it should not get an identical
  post.
