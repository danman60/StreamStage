# Recital campaign — Meta ad copy

Drafted 2026-08-31. Structure per the StudioSage ads template (Headline / Primary text / Link
description / CTA / creative pairing). Rules: no em or en dashes, no invented numbers, quotes
verbatim with name and studio, link always streamstage.live/recitals, revenue line stays a
reframe. Early Bird Dec 31 2026 is the only date allowed and only appears in Concept 5.

All ads: CTA button **Learn More** → `https://streamstage.live/recitals`.
Ad account naming: `Recital-2026 / <concept> / <variant>`.

**The four offer pillars (Daniel, 2026-08-31) — every long primary text carries at least two,
and no piece of campaign copy may contradict them:**
1. Livestream
2. Multiple media options (video, photography, livestream — mix or bundle)
3. The branded portal (studio-branded, families buy from it directly)
4. **10-day turnaround** (the one clients love; the number is TEN days, never "1 week")

---

## Concept 1 — The revenue reframe

**Creative:** static R1 (type-led) or `proof-wide-vs-close-9x16.mp4`

- **Headline A:** Your recital video can pay you
- **Headline B:** Stop paying for your recital video
- **Headline C:** The media fee is yours to set

**Primary text (short):**
You charge the media fee. We bill per dancer. The difference is yours. Recital video, livestream
and photography, priced per dancer and published, not quoted.

**Primary text (long):**
Most studios treat the recital video as a cost. It only works that way because nobody set it up
otherwise. Here is the other way: you set the media fee your families pay, we bill you a flat
published per dancer rate, and the difference stays with the studio. Video, photography and
livestream, one team, one night, delivered through a portal branded for your studio within 10
days. There is a calculator that shows your number in about a minute. Nothing to book and nobody
calls you.

**Link description:** Published per dancer pricing. See your number in a minute.

## Concept 2 — Wide vs close (the proof pair)

**Creative:** `proof-wide-vs-close-9x16.mp4` (REQUIRED — the copy references the footage)

- **Headline A:** One camera at the back. Or this.
- **Headline B:** Same routine. Same night. Two videos.
- **Headline C:** This is why families buy the video

**Primary text (short):**
Same routine, same stage, same moment. Left: one locked camera at the back of the room. Right:
what we capture. That gap is the entire reason families buy the video.

**Primary text (long):**
Watch both sides. One is a camera on a tripod at the back of the house: heads, a distant stage,
and a parent squinting for their kid. The other is the same routine shot properly, where you can
see the face she makes when she lands it. Families do not buy a recording. They buy that face.
Multi camera video, livestream and photography, priced per dancer, published rates.

**Link description:** See the difference, then see your pricing.

## Variant discipline

Launch = 1 campaign, one ad set per audience (see cowork prompt), 5 concepts × Headline A only.
Add B/C headlines as refresh when frequency climbs or CTR sags. Never run two concepts with the
same creative file in one ad set.
