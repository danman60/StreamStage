# Content day campaign, caption bank

Drafted 2026-08-17. Sixteen slots, four weeks, repeats indefinitely.
Assets and constraints per `ASSET-MANIFEST.md` and `contentday-campaign-PLAYBOOK.md`.

**Rules applied to every caption below:**

- **Evergreen. No months, no seasons, no deadlines, no countdowns.** If a date appears, the post
  stops being reusable and the rotation starts needing maintenance.
- No em dashes or en dashes.
- No invented statistics, client counts or prices.
- Quotes are verbatim from `contentday/quotes/quote-sheet.md`. Consent is handled, names run
  attached.
- **Never post the five `example-*.mp4` clips.** They are other accounts' work: @joffreyballet,
  @imaginedancechallenge, @ikindance, @theballeteducator, @moveitshakeit. They are briefing
  reference only.
- No recital material here. No media fee, no per dancer pricing, no recital day quotes.
- Link in bio and in stories is `streamstage.live/contentday`. Never the builder URL.

**Standard tag block:** `#dancestudioowner #danceteacher #studiomarketing #dancestudio
#contentcreation #ontariodance`
