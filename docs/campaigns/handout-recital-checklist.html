The recital day checklist

What we ask for, what we bring, and what your families end up with. Written for the studio owner who is running the day, not for a video crew. Use it even if you never hire us.

Before the show, from you

  • Show date and venue confirmed
  • Number of dancers, so pricing is settled
  • Running order, even a draft one
  • Number of shows and rough call times
  • Who the day-of contact is, and their phone
  • Anything you want filmed that is not on stage
  • Any dancer who must not appear on camera

Planning time with you is included before the show. It is where most of the problems on this page get solved.

Venue questions worth asking early

  • Where can cameras sit in the house
  • Is there a balcony or a raised position
  • Can we take a feed from the house sound desk
  • Power at the camera positions
  • Load in time and door access
  • Does the venue restrict filming or streaming

The sound feed is the one people forget. Audience audio and stage audio are not the same thing, and parents notice.

The week of

  • Final running order sent
  • Tell families the video exists and how they get it
  • Decide whether you are streaming, and to whom
  • Confirm the day-of contact has not changed

What we bring

  • Multiple cameras, full stage plus the angles that catch faces
  • Our own operators, so none of your team is holding a camera
  • All audio and video gear, nothing for you to rent or borrow
  • Livestream for the family who cannot be in the room
  • Photography the same night, same team

What your families get

  • A link, not a disc
  • A portal branded for your studio
  • They find their dancer's routine, not the whole three hour show
  • Watch it, download it, keep it
  • Ten day turnaround

The part most studios miss

You set the media fee your families pay. We bill you per dancer. The difference is yours.

There is a calculator on our page that works it out with your own numbers in about a minute. Nothing to book, no account, nobody calls you.

Booking reality

  • Spring recitals land on the same handful of Saturdays
  • Crews are finite, so two studios cannot have the same date
  • December shows usually have more room
