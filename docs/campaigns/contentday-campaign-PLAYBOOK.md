## What evergreen changes

An evergreen campaign has no deadline, which removes the easiest lever and forces the harder one.
The old version leaned on "October is filling up." Nothing replaces that, and nothing should: a
manufactured deadline on an evergreen offer is a lie the buyer can check.

What carries it instead is the standing argument, which is strong enough on its own:

**Message spine** (from `talk1-video-script.md`, stage-tested):
do the hard thing once · shoot, edit, post, repeat is why posting stops in February · piecemeal
content looks piecemeal · one clip, nine posts · pro feeds UGC, it does not replace it.

Practically, evergreen means every asset must survive being posted in any month. No dates in
captions, no "this fall", no countdowns, no seasonal hooks. Write it once, run it all year, refresh
when the footage changes rather than when the calendar turns.

## The one thing this campaign has to do

Get a studio owner to picture a full year of content coming out of one morning, then send them to
the builder to price it themselves. Not to book on the page. To **price it**, which is the small
commitment that starts the conversation.
