One morning of shooting, planned properly, is worth more than a year of grabbing phone clips between classes. This is the sheet we work off. Use it even if you shoot it yourself.

Decide first

  • Which classes are on camera, and at what time
  • Which teachers are comfortable being filmed
  • Whether the owner goes on camera
  • Costumes, uniforms or studio wear
  • Any dancer who must not appear on camera
  • What the studio needs to look like before we arrive

The most common regret is not booking enough classes into the morning. Empty rooms film fast, full rooms do not.

Five formats worth having on file

  • Day in the life, one class start to finish
  • Interview, a teacher or the owner talking straight to camera
  • Mic'd up, the real coaching voice over real class footage
  • POV, the room from a dancer's eyeline
  • The correction, the moment a note lands and the dancer fixes it

All five can be shot in the same morning. Filming them one at a time across a season is why they never get made.

The shot list nobody writes down

  • The front door and the sign
  • The studio empty, lights up
  • Hands, feet, mirrors, barre
  • The waiting area with parents in it
  • A teacher demonstrating, not just watching
  • Something that only happens at your studio

One clip, nine posts

Reel, hook first Carousel, teaching point Story set, behind the scenes Class promo Teacher spotlight Registration push Website background loop Ad creative Filler for a quiet week

This is the whole economics of a content day. The shoot is the small part.

What you walk away with

  • A promo film
  • Short social cuts
  • All the raw footage, yours to mine

Studios tell us the raw is what they keep going back to. Tiffany Adoranti at Caledonia School of Dance looks at hers about once a week to see what she can pull out and post that day.

Keep posting your own phone videos

Pro footage does not replace what your teachers and dancers film. It feeds it.

Phone content is immediate and honest, and it dries up the week everyone gets busy. Only one of the two can be stockpiled in advance.

After the shoot

  • Pick your promo cut length
  • Decide who holds the raw footage at your end
  • Put a standing hour in the calendar to mine it
  • Post from it before you shoot anything new
