# Talk 2 — proposed running order (38 → 28 slides, honest 60 minutes)

Working file: `expo-assets/decks/talk2-ai.html` (38 slides, phone controller wired, newest copy).
Nothing implemented yet — this is the shape for you to approve or redline.

## The diagnosis in one line

The deck promises five free moves on slide 6 and doesn't start paying them until slide 12.
Everything else follows from that: the 101 block is front-loaded as theory before anyone has done
anything, the agentic act sits between the moves and the product, and the product itself doesn't
appear until slide 30 of 38. You then had to cut ~33 minutes live, on your feet, which is why
Routine 436, the poster beat and the dashboard walkthrough vanished and the demo got squeezed.

## The three structural moves

1. **Pay the promise immediately.** Move 1 lands at minute 6, not minute 25.
2. **Distribute the 101.** No theory act. Each move carries the one concept it needs, at the moment
   it needs it — "project/thread" arrives when you build the folder, not twenty minutes earlier.
3. **Caveats after competence, not before.** Confidently-wrong / car-wash / trust-but-verify moves
   *down* next to "where AI should not replace you." A warning lands once someone has felt the thing
   work; up front it just adds fear to a room you spent five minutes de-fearing.
