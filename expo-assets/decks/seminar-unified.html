Pre-flight — run it twice.

T−24 hours (hotel room)

Auto-updates OFF — nothing installs overnight.
Everything local — deck + reel MP4 on laptop AND USB.
Airplane-mode test — wifi off, arrow every slide, run every beat.
Rehearsal text — onboard + ask a seeded question, confirm instant answer.
Charge laptop, phone, clicker (+ spares), power bank.
Print run-of-show ×2, handouts, failure playbook.

T−30 minutes (at the podium)

Projector — 1920×1080 mirror, edges not cropped (read the densest slide from the back).
Audio — play 5s of the reel THROUGH THE PA (HDMI audio is the classic silent failure).
Notifications OFF — Do Not Disturb, no calendar popups on stage.
Browser 100% zoom, bookmarks hidden, fullscreen tested.
Hotspot on as hot spare; load the wall once now.
Second rehearsal text — confirms it’s live NOW. Then phone → DND.
