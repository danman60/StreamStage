Appendix · press A to get here · → at the end of the next slide returns you
Why one 30-second ad becomes six exports.
Open gate

Shoot the whole square

Open gate records the full sensor — roughly 4:3, like a 90s TV. From that one file you can pull a full-resolution 9:16 and a full-resolution 16:9. You frame it once and decide the shape later.

What cropping costs

4K in, about 720 out

Shoot 16:9, then crop to vertical, and you keep only the narrow strip of pixels in the middle. You shot 4K and you are delivering something closer to 720. It was never a resolution problem — it is a framing problem.

Six deliveries

Same 30 seconds, six exports

Feed, story and reel, desktop, mobile, YouTube, paid. Different shapes, different safe areas, different places the caption can sit. One edit, six renders — that is the actual work nobody warns you about.

Codecs

H.264 vs H.265

H.264 plays on everything. H.265 is roughly half the file at the same quality and still is not universal — some phones and some older machines will not decode it. If you are handing a file to a stranger, send H.264.

And the specs change roughly every six months. That is not you failing to keep up — that is the job.

Appendix · if you are shooting it yourself
Camera settings, short version.
Frame rate

30 fps

24 is the movie-theatre look. 60 is more frames in a second — that is not the same as more quality. 30 is the one that survives every platform.

Resolution

4K

Resolution is your crop budget. Shoot bigger than you deliver and you can reframe, punch in, and cut a vertical later without paying for it.

Exposure

Everything on auto

Auto white balance, auto ISO, auto f-stop. A correctly exposed auto shot beats a manual one you got wrong, and you are there to run a studio, not to chase a light meter.

Variety

Vary the focal length

Wide, close, from the floor, at the toe. Every shot taken from the same doorway at shoulder height is exactly what makes UGC look like UGC.

On a phone

Zoom with your feet

A phone has one lens worth using. Move closer instead of pinching — pinching just throws away pixels you already paid for.

Vertical or landscape? If you cannot shoot open gate, shoot a mix. Short answer: do both.
