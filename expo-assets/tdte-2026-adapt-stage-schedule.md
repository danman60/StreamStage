## Session descriptions (verbatim, for the deliverable pages)

**An Honest Conversation Around Dance — Live Podcast with Jason Pickett and Guests.** Join Jason Pickett, host of the No Starving Artists Podcast, for a live conversation exploring the evolving world of dance and dance education. Session presented by Dance Attack.

**Command the Stage/Room — Kim McSwain.** Led by industry mogul Kim McSwain, this powerful experience helps dancers develop confidence, presence, and the ability to walk into any room or onto any stage with purpose. Through mindset, performance, and intentional training habits, dancers are challenged with the questions we often lose sight of in today's industry. Drawing from her work with some of the most iconic U.S. tours and global artists, Kim delivers an in-person experience unlike any other — grounded in excellence, discipline, and heart. Session presented by International Competitive Dancer Registry (ICDR).

**Advocacy in Dance: Supporting Youth — Michelle Crossman.** Challenging the binary of dance, and learning to expand our knowledge around safer spaces for youth in our industry. We will cover how we can better support LGBTQ2S+ and other marginalized youth in our own classes, studios and more.

**Setting the Tone: Building a Culture of Connection, Confidence, and Care — Barb Jackman.** This presentation will provide practical tools for building trust, encouraging confidence, and creating a culture that inspires people to thrive both in and out of the studio. Session presented by Adapt Syllabus.

**Know Who's On Stage. Know Who's Watching. Verified Identities & Privacy Protection by ICDR.** You've heard the conversations—DMs, backstage whispers, post-weekend concerns. It's time to bring them into the open. Safety. Fairness. Transparency. Be part of it. Join ICDR staff and special guests for a live discussion where your questions help shape what's next for dance. With free participation and real digital protections through a shared verified ID system, there's every reason to get involved. This isn't just a presentation—it's your chance to build a safer, more transparent industry. Bonus: Attendees receive an extra entry into the One Me | One ID Prize Campaign—over $75,000 in prizes. Panelists: Jamie Hodgins, Tara Pickford, Kim McSwain, Bryce Hunter and Jason Pickett. Session presented by ICDR.

**Why AI? Save Your Studio Time, Money, and Stress. — Daniel Abrahamson.** Studio owners are buried in repetitive tasks: parent emails, reminders, newsletters, admin follow-up, scheduling questions, and the constant pressure to respond quickly. In this practical session, technologist Daniel Abrahamson shows how dance studios can use AI to reduce menial work, save staff time, and create better systems without losing the personal voice of the studio. Daniel will walk through real-world examples including parent-email FAQ bots, AI-assisted newsletters, automated follow-up workflows, and human-in-the-loop systems that keep the studio owner in control. This session is designed for non-technical studio owners who want useful, approachable ways to make technology work for their business. Walk away with a clearer understanding of where AI can help, where it should not replace a human, and how to start using automation to win back hours every week. Session presented by StreamStage.

**Creating a Culture of Confidence — Elina Bagshaw.** Discover how to build a confident, resilient studio culture using five practical pillars: a shared language, a positive mindset woven into daily teaching, consistent rituals, parent partnership, and leadership-driven priorities. This session provides a framework for creating an environment where confidence becomes an inherent part of the studio. Session presented by Own the Stage.

**From Stages to Pages — Shereeka Gray.** You've survived the pivots, worn every hat, and kept showing up for your students through everything the last few years have thrown at the dance world. But what if everything you've built, every skill, every program, every moment of connection, was preparing you for something even bigger? In this talk-show style conversation, children's book author, curriculum creator, and arts educator Shereeka Gray shares how she turned 20+ years in the studio into a book series, a school programming brand, and a creative legacy, and why your expertise has far more range than the dance world has ever given you credit for. This one's for every dance educator who has ever wondered — who said I could only have one dream?

**The Ripple Effect — Barb Jackman.** A presentation on learning how intentional planning and purposeful leadership can lead to a more successful and rewarding season for everyone. Session presented by Adapt Syllabus.

**Lead the Room — Kim McSwain.** Led by industry mogul Kim McSwain, this transformative leadership intensive is designed for studio owners and educators ready to inspire at a higher level. Focused on leadership, communication, team culture, and commanding a room with confidence, this one-of-a-kind workshop challenges teachers to reflect on and remember the steps that built community within those one freshly painted studio walls. Through Kim's unmatched industry experience and signature approach, educators learn how to elevate morale, guide with purpose, and build thriving artists and teams. Session presented by ICDR.

**Managing Finances for Entrepreneurs — Nick Eddy.** Whether you're running a dance studio, teaching classes, freelancing, or looking to sharpen your financial skills, this engaging seminar is packed with practical tools to help you thrive. Learn budgeting essentials, taxes, bookkeeping, banking strategies, and healthy money management habits—all tailored to the realities of working in the arts. Session presented by Creative Arts Financial, First Ontario Credit Union.

**Cocktails & Costumes — Fashion Show.** Experience the excitement as the dance industry's top costume and dancewear brands come together for one unforgettable runway event. Cocktails and Costumes showcases the latest collections in recital costumes, performance wear, and studio apparel, featuring dancers of all ages. (Description truncated in source paste.)
