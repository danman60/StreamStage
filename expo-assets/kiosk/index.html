Both devices have to be on the same network. Use a travel router or the laptop’s own hotspot. It does not need internet — only each other.

  2. Fire Stick Silk: the address above, then bookmark it
  3. Tablet: the same address ending in /tablet

If a film will not play

Run ./expo-assets/kiosk/sync-media.sh — it re-copies the five films and always prefers the newest cut. A product whose film is missing does not black out: it goes straight to its signup QR. Full detail is in README-BOOTH.md.
Two things are still assumptions. The CompSync QR points at compsync.net because no signup URL was ever given, and Reflect’s line is taken verbatim from its own film script rather than confirmed by you. Both are noted in README-BOOTH.md.
